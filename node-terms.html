Jobs

Jobs allow any user or Service Provider to advertise a vacancy. NODE hosts the advertisement only — we do not participate in, mediate, or take responsibility for the hiring process. All communication regarding a Job advertisement — including applications, enquiries and correspondence — is directed externally to the listing organisation or company, via the email address, website, or contact details the lister has provided. Classifieds

Classifieds allow the advertising of goods or items for sale, exchange, or giveaway. Where payment is required to publish a Classified, the post is released and made publicly visible only once payment has been confirmed by our payment processor.

All communication regarding a Classified — including enquiries, negotiation, and arrangement of exchange or collection — is conducted externally, through a third-party communication channel of the users' own choosing (for example, WhatsApp, SMS, a phone call, or another messaging application). NODE's internal messaging feature is not used for Classifieds. NODE has no visibility into, control over, or responsibility for any communication that takes place through these external channels.
