What is NODE

One app. Every trusted local service.

NODE is a local services marketplace where communities find trusted, verified service providers — plumbers, hairdressers, caterers, drivers, tutors and everyone in between — all searchable, reviewable, and reachable by direct message, in one place. No more relying on a WhatsApp group that scrolls away, or a Facebook post nobody follows up on.

Why NODE exists

You've felt this problem before.

Before NODE

You need a plumber. You ask around. Someone gives you a number. They don't answer. You try someone else. Three days later, you're still without hot water — and you still don't actually know if they're any good.

With NODE

You open the app, search by category, and see real profiles with real reviews from people in your own community. You message them directly, see their reply instantly, and book with confidence — all before you've left the couch.

How it works

Four steps. That's it.

1

Browse

Search by category or keyword — find exactly who you need, nearby.

2

Message

Chat directly in the app. No swapping numbers, no leaving NODE.

3

Book

Confirm the job, agree the details, all inside the conversation.

4

Review

Rate your experience — helping the next person in your community decide.

What NODE makes possible

Illustrative stories, built from real conversations.

The names below are illustrative — imagined from the kinds of stories we hear every week from local business owners. Real NODE success stories, with real names, coming soon.

THE WELDER

Solomon learned welding from his father, who learned it from his father. Three generations of skill — hidden in a converted garage, surviving on whatever work a neighbour mentioned over the fence.

Three days on NODE, the first message came in. Within three months, he'd hired his brother and moved into a real workshop.

"NODE didn't give me talent. I already had that. NODE gave me an audience."

THE HAIRDRESSER

Nomsa didn't have a salon. She had a bag of scissors, and a habit of knocking on doors — leaving a scrap of paper with her number, hoping it would survive the wind, the wash, the bin.

A year later, a client who'd lost that very paper found her again on NODE. Today, Nomsa runs a two-chair salon, employing two women she trained herself.

"I knocked on a thousand doors. NODE opened them all at once."

THE MECHANIC

Jacob fixed cars in the same small workshop for nineteen years — trusted by everyone in his hundred-person town, invisible to everyone else.

Within months of joining NODE, bookings came from towns he'd never worked in. Today he runs a full workshop with two employees.

"I almost gave up. I'm glad someone showed me NODE."
